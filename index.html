The "Summer" edition features a rear glass panel that absorbs ambient light to trickle-charge the battery. In peak summer, it can extend battery life by 4 hours daily.

A hidden detail in the chassis allows for "Breeze-In" cooling—micro-vents that open only when the phone detects high external temperatures during summer months.
